Serbo-Croatian

Etymology

From po- +‎ nositi.

Pronunciation

  • IPA(key): /ponǒsiti/
  • Hyphenation: po‧no‧si‧ti

Verb

ponòsiti impf (Cyrillic spelling поно̀сити)

  1. (reflexive, with instrumental) to be proud, pride oneself on
